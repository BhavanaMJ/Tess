# TESS — PHASE 3.4 USER GUIDE
## Smart Capture: ChatGPT-Assisted Garment Import Workflow

Welcome to the new **AI Garment Catalogue & Metadata Import** experience in TESS. This guide explains how to use your dedicated ChatGPT Project to generate studio catalogue images and comprehensive garment attributes, then import them into your TESS wardrobe.

---

### Step 1: In Your Dedicated ChatGPT Project
1. Open ChatGPT on your computer or phone and select your dedicated project:
   **`TESS — AI GARMENT CATALOGUE & METADATA GENERATOR`**
2. Upload a clear photograph of your garment laid flat or hanging.
3. ChatGPT will:
   - Isolate the garment from shadows and background clutter.
   - Generate a clean, high-resolution **studio catalogue image** with a neutral background.
   - Produce a code block containing **Schema Version 1.1 JSON metadata** capturing all 12 attribute sections (colours, neckline, fabric, size, care, brand, etc.).
4. Save the generated studio image to your phone gallery (or downloads folder) and copy the JSON code block to your clipboard.

---

### Step 2: In TESS Mobile App

#### Opening the Import Wizard
1. Open the TESS app and navigate to **My Wardrobe**.
2. Tap the **+** (Add Garment) button.
3. Select **AI Garment Catalogue Import (ChatGPT-Assisted)**.

---

#### Stage 1: Prepare Garment
- *(Optional)* Attach your original phone photograph from Gallery or Camera. This preserves your original photo in TESS as a reference asset.
- *(Optional)* Enter any personal notes (e.g., *"Purchased in Jaipur, dry clean only"*).
- Tap **Continue**.

---

#### Stage 2: Import Studio Catalogue Image
- Tap **Choose from Gallery** (or drag & drop on Web).
- Select the clean studio catalogue image generated by ChatGPT.
- Verify the studio quality preview.
- Tap **Continue**.

---

#### Stage 3: Import Metadata JSON
- Tap **Paste JSON** to automatically insert the JSON from your clipboard.
- *(Optional)* Tap **Format** to clean up indentation.
- Tap **Validate Schema 1.1**.
- TESS will instantly verify all 12 sections:
  - If any syntax error exists, an informative red banner highlights the exact line.
  - If valid, green success badges indicate validation passed.
- Tap **Review Garment**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Flexible Category Support**: ChatGPT frequently outputs category variants such as `Bottomwear`, `Pants`, `Trousers`, or `Topwear`. TESS automatically normalizes these to canonical categories (`Bottoms`, `Tops`, `Ethnic Wear`, etc.) while preserving your exact subcategory (such as `Lounge Pants`, `Jeans`, `Kurta`) and garment type descriptions intact!

---

#### Stage 4: Review & Register
- **Visual Assets**: View your high-resolution studio catalogue image side-by-side with your original photo.
- **Attributes Review**: Inspect collapsible cards:
  - *Appearance & Colours* (primary/secondary colours, pattern, texture)
  - *Construction & Fit* (neckline, sleeve length, fit, silhouette)
  - *Fabric & Composition* (fabric appearance, material, composition)
  - *Sizing & Brand* (labelled size, brand)
  - *Usage, Occasions & Seasons* (occasions, seasons)
  - *Care & Physical Location* (washing method, storage location)
- **Inline Editing**: Tap the pencil icon next to any attribute to edit or correct values prior to registration.
- **Add to My Wardrobe**: Tap **Add to My Wardrobe**, review the confirmation summary, and tap **Confirm & Register**.
- Your garment is assigned a unique sequential ID (e.g. `CLO-019`) and added to your wardrobe inventory with all metadata preserved!
