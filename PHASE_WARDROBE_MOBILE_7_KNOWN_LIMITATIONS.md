# Phase 7: Known Limitations & Future Roadmap Deferred Scope

### 1. Scope Boundaries for Phase 7

1. **Wishlist & Shopping Gap Integration (Deferred to Phase 8)**:
   - While Capsule Wardrobe Intelligence identifies category and palette gaps, automatic creation of wishlist items and dynamic merchant price comparison belong to Phase 8 ("Wardrobe Wishlist & Shopping Intelligence").
2. **Multi-Currency Normalization**:
   - Items purchased in foreign currencies (e.g. USD, EUR) are currently tracked with their native currency identifier. Real-time forex conversion against a user base currency is deferred to Phase 8.
3. **Complex Multi-Pattern AI Clashing Metrics**:
   - Capsule compatibility currently evaluates category pairings (e.g. Tops + Bottoms) and color compatibility. Advanced generative aesthetic scoring across micro-patterns (plaid vs. houndstooth) will be expanded in future AI models.
4. **Historical Depreciation Curves**:
   - Cost-per-wear calculates straight-line nominal CPW ($\text{price} / \text{wears}$). Secondary market resale value depreciation curves (e.g., Poshmark/The RealReal valuation models) are not calculated.
